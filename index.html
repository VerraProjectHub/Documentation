info Platform Overview

The Verra Project Hub is a comprehensive platform designed to streamline the management and tracking of carbon market projects. It provides tools and resources for project developers, validators, and stakeholders to efficiently navigate the complexities of carbon credit standards including VCS (Verified Carbon Standard), CCB (Climate, Community & Biodiversity), and PWRP (Plastic Waste Reduction Program).

Key Features

  • Project registration and management
  • Document submission and tracking
  • Validation and verification workflows
  • Credit issuance monitoring
  • Stakeholder collaboration tools

play_arrow Getting Started

Begin your journey with the Verra Project Hub by following these essential steps. Whether you're a new project developer or an experienced validator, this guide will help you navigate the platform effectively.

1. Account Setup & Access

Create your Verra account and configure your profile with the necessary credentials and organizational information. Ensure you have the appropriate permissions for your role in the carbon market ecosystem.

2. Project Registration

Learn how to register new projects, select appropriate methodologies, and submit initial documentation. The platform guides you through each step of the registration process with built-in validation checks.

3. Document Management

Master the document submission system, track review statuses, and respond to validator feedback efficiently. The system supports various document types and provides automated notifications for status changes.

verified Supported Standards

The Project Hub supports multiple Verra standards, each designed to address specific aspects of climate action and sustainable development.

VCS Program

The Verified Carbon Standard program for greenhouse gas emission reduction and removal projects.

CCB Standards

Climate, Community & Biodiversity standards for projects with multiple environmental and social benefits.

PWRP

Plastic Waste Reduction Program addressing the global plastic pollution crisis.
